Tamanna

Health economist and public health researcher · Dhaka, Bangladesh

Hi, I am Tamanna, a health economist and public health researcher based in Dhaka. I hold an MSS and a BSS in Health Economics from the University of Dhaka, and I work as a Research Assistant at ARCED Foundation, where I help run experimental and field-based studies with researchers from the World Bank, IFPRI, UC San Diego, Tufts, Stanford and Georgetown. You can view my CV here.

At ARCED I contribute to a customer feedback experiment on women's bus travel in Dhaka with the World Bank, a field experiment and follow-up survey on air pollution and workers' productivity with UC San Diego and Tufts, and a study of trade with multinational buyers and greener production in the Bangladeshi fashion industry with IFPRI and BIDS. I also worked on an audit study of the electric three-wheeler battery market with researchers at Stanford and Georgetown.

My day-to-day work covers the full survey cycle: designing and programming questionnaires, training and supervising enumerators, monitoring data quality in real time, and analysing the data in Stata. Across six field surveys of about 5,000 respondents I have trained more than 100 enumerators, supervisors, back-checkers and audio auditors. I have also conducted 7 focus group discussions and 4 in-depth interviews under participant-safety protocols.

Before ARCED, I was a research intern at two divisions of icddr,b and a research assistant at the BRAC James P Grant School of Public Health. My research interests lie in health economics, child health, public health expenditure, and gender and transport safety. I plan to pursue advanced study and build a research career in evidence-based health policy.
