I am a health economics researcher working at the interface of public health policy, health systems and field-based evidence. I design and run surveys, experiments and qualitative studies in Bangladesh, with a focus on child health, environmental health and women's mobility.

About

Background, training and goals

My training is in health economics, with a particular interest in public health research, health systems, environmental health and policy. I completed both my bachelor's and master's degrees at the University of Dhaka, with coursework in applied econometrics, health economics, epidemiology and research methodology.

Since 2022 I have worked in applied research at icddr,b, the BRAC James P Grant School of Public Health and ARCED Foundation, where I am a Research Assistant. My work covers the full research cycle: designing and programming surveys, training and supervising field teams, monitoring data quality, conducting qualitative fieldwork, and analysing data in Stata.

I plan to pursue advanced study and build a research career in health economics and evidence-based health policy.
